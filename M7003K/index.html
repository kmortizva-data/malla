Module 0 · any time

Assignments, old exams and protocols

Period 1 assignments: product balance, limiting grades and selectivity, mass and water balance, plus the computer lab on mass balancing. Period 2: revenues, cost analysis, technical-economical calculations. Old written exams 2014-2022 (4 hours, calculator, about 7 questions: essays on flotation, AG mills, SLon and gold, a true/false block, a flotation-circuit mass balance and a dilution-ratio water balance) with one suggested solution.

quiz None pts

Introduction

Course introduction (2026-09-01): aims, content of the two periods, teachers, examination modules (written exam Q1 2.4 cr, oral exam Q2 3 cr, lab and assignments 1.5 cr, field trip 0.6 cr), study trip in week 40. Formula compilation and slurry density/dilution ratio sheets.
