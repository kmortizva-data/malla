Module 6 practice quiz: proxies, tests and the GCT

10 questions drawn at random from a bank of 20, 20 minutes, the explanation after every answer. Same shape as the Canvas quiz; only the questions are ours.

Sources: Canvas M6 Core concepts — proxies, throughput tests and test programmes (measurement vs test, the four levels, proxies, the six questions, the GCT), Canvas M6 check yourself (the eight statements), The GCT laboratory instruction and Mwanga, Rosenkranz and Lamberg 2017 as summarised on the Canvas pages, The Assignment 3 guide

Before you start

Write down the score you expect. The gap between expected and actual, in either direction, tells you more than the score itself. That is the course's own advice.
