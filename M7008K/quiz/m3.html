Module 3 practice quiz: matching the question to the instrument

10 questions drawn at random from a bank of 22, 20 minutes, the explanation after every answer. Same shape as the Canvas quiz; only the questions are ours.

Sources: Canvas M3 Core concepts — which method answers which question (the comparison table), Canvas M3 check yourself (the eight statements), Characterisation methods — main lecture (handout), Whiten 2007 and Kojonen & Johanson 1998 as listed on the M3 material page

Before you start

Write down the score you expect. The gap between expected and actual, in either direction, tells you more than the score itself. That is the course's own advice.
