Module 8 practice quiz: reading an economic model

10 questions drawn at random from a bank of 22, 20 minutes, the explanation after every answer. Same shape as the Canvas quiz; only the questions are ours.

Sources: Canvas M8 Core concepts, what makes a block ore (the value chain, the net smelter return and its four deductions, the two cut-off grades, net present value, the worked production year and the geometallurgical twist), Canvas M8 check yourself (the eight statements), The PC-lab economic appraisal instruction on Canvas, The lecture Economic Appraisal_2026.pptx (value chain, resource and reserve, dilution and ore loss, cost estimation, taxes, working capital, NPV and IRR, risk diagrams), Boliden Summary Report, Resources and Reserves 2022, Aitik, for one realistic example of a variable cut-off

Before you start

Write down the score you expect. The gap between expected and actual, in either direction, tells you more than the score itself. That is the course's own advice.
