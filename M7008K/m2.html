Sampling and sampling theory

Every number you will use for the rest of this course — every assay, every mineral grade, every work index — is a measurement made on a few hundred grams that stand in for a few million tonnes. If that stand-in is wrong, nothing downstream can repair it: the most careful mineralogy in the world on a biased sample gives a confident wrong answer.
