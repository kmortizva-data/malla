Modelling, simulation and process metallurgy

Everything you have done so far produces numbers about rock: assays, modal mineralogy, liberation, a work index, a recovery from a bench test. This module is where those numbers are finally made to do something. A flowsheet model turns ore properties into plant performance — tonnes per hour, kilowatt-hours per tonne, per cent recovery, per cent concentrate grade — one ore domain at a time.
