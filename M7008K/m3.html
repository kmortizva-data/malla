Characterisation methods and tools

M1 told you a geometallurgical model needs mineral grades, textures and hardness, and M2 told you how to get a defensible sample. This module is about the instruments in between — and about one habit that separates a useful characterisation programme from an expensive one. The habit is to start from the question, not from the instrument.
