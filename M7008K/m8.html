Economic appraisal

A block of rock is not ore because of what it contains. It is ore because someone can make money out of it, and "someone can make money out of it" is a calculation with a definite answer. This module is that calculation: what the payable metal is worth, what is deducted before you see any of it, what it costs to mine and mill, and where the line falls between ore and waste.
