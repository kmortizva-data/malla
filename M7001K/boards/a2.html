Assignment 2: every calibration run, ranked

On this page you can see every simulation that was run to calibrate the rod mill, the pebble mill, the cyclone and the whole circuit of Assignment 2. The runs were made with MODSIM's own calculation engine, called directly without opening the graphical interface, which made it possible to test hundreds of parameter sets in a few minutes. Each board ranks the runs from best to worst with a score that is explained right above its table, and a second board shows how much each parameter moves the result. The figures and the flowsheet for the report should still come from the GUI, running the calibrated values.

This table shows the 15 best of the 1627 runs, ordered from the lowest score to the highest. Each run is a complete simulation of the unit, fed with the feed that was measured in the plant. The score starts from the size curves of the underflow and the overflow, compared at the six sieves. It then adds half of the error in underflow solids, in per cent of the measured 76.0 t/h, and a quarter of the error in underflow dilution, in per cent of the measured 0.308. What the colours mean
  • Dilution ratio. The dilution ratio is the number of tonnes of water that travel with each tonne of solids. The cell is green when the simulated ratio is within 5 % of the sheet's value, which is the same assumption as for the flows.
  • Cyclone pressure drop. The sheet says that the cyclone works at about 100 kPa, so the cell is green between 75 and 125 kPa. The score also adds 0.05 for every kPa outside that band. That way, a cyclone that fits the size curves only at an unrealistic pressure cannot come out on top.
  • Size curve fit. For each run, the simulated size curve is compared with the measured one at the six sieves of the sheet, and the differences are combined into one number, their root mean square, in percentage points. The cell is green at 2.0 points or less. Two points is roughly how much two sieve analyses of the same sample can differ, so a run inside that band fits about as well as the data allow. The threshold is an assumption, because the sheet does not give one.
  • Roping test. A cyclone ropes when its underflow comes out as a thick rope instead of a spray, and when that happens the separation becomes coarser. MODSIM checks for it with two published criteria, one by Mular and Jull and one by Concha. The cell is green when the cyclone passes both. Because the cyclone in the sampled plant was working normally, the score adds 2 to any run that ropes.
  • Underflow solids. The cell is green when the simulated underflow carries within 5 % of the 76.0 t/h that was measured in the plant. Five per cent is about the accuracy of a flow measurement in a running plant, and it is an assumption.
  • Verdict. The green cell marks the best run of the ranking, which is the one that goes on to the next stage of the work.
