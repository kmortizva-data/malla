MODSIM exercises and their guides

try first, then compare with the worked PAK and the suggested solution

The course numbers these 1, 3 and 4; there is no Exercise 2 in Canvas.

Exercise 1: design of a screening station

Three fractions (>100, 20–100, <20 mm) from Kirunavaara D-ore at 600 t/h with Karra screen models; middle fraction to crushing, coarse + fines to the AG mills. Sensitivity: move the coarse deck in 5 mm steps.

Read the guided walkthrough

Exercise 3: comparison of crusher models

A test circuit per crusher family, one machine per model in parallel from a common split feed (Kiruna D-ore, 100 t/h per machine, RRS d₆₃.₂ 100 mm, n = 1). Jaw OSS 50 mm; gyratory OSS 50 mm; cone, roll and HPGR CSS 25 mm. Sensitivity: +50 % feed rate.

Read the guided walkthrough

Exercise 4: open and closed tertiary circuit

Secondary-crushed Kiruna D-ore, 600 t/h, RRS d₆₃.₂ 15 mm, n 0.9, Wi 12 kWh/t. Deliver d₈₀ ≈ 5 mm and/or d₉₉ ≈ 10 mm without choked crushing, open and then closed over a Karra screen. Compare feed curves to primary grinding and total kWh/t including a mill to d₈₀ 0.1 mm.

Read the guided walkthrough
