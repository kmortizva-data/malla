Assessment
  • Assignments 3.5 cr
  • Seminars 3.0 cr
  • Quizzes 1.0 cr

Grade limits on total points: grade 3: 50 % · grade 4: 65 % · grade 5: 80 %.

Quizzes: Ten questions drawn at random from the module's question bank on every attempt. Read the explanation after each question, even when right. Write down the score you expect before starting.

The course's AI rule, kept here on purpose

AI may be used as a language aid (grammar, spelling, phrasing), not to produce analysis, results or conclusions. State purpose and extent of AI use in the submission. Be able to explain and defend every statement at the seminar.

Malla is a study tool: it prepares you with practice exercises, practice quizzes and primers. The assignment analysis is the group's own work.
